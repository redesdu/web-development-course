- Stakes: ungraded, anonymous, browser-local; no analytics or instructor reporting

## Practice objectives

Students can:

1. Retrieve compact selector syntax and HTML element roles.
2. Classify HTML/CSS responsibilities and selector matches.
3. Distinguish spacing, visibility, Flexbox, and Grid cases.
4. Produce a defensible structure-and-style plan for a new interface.

## Format rationale and progression

1. Classification activates the HTML/CSS responsibility boundary.
2. Five flashcards support attempted recall of compact selector-symbol pairs.
3. One MCQ tests GET/POST discrimination where plausible alternatives reveal a misconception.
4. Classification tests selector matching from concrete markup.
5. Classification tests layout, spacing, and visibility decisions.
6. A structured short application requires production in a fresh search-interface case.

MCQ is deliberately limited to the form-method decision. Recognition cannot satisfy the production objective. Flashcards are self-checks, not grades or spaced repetition.

## Feedback policy

- Learners commit before seeing the explanation.
- Correct and incorrect paths explain the governing rule.
- Resolved feedback remains visible until explicit continuation.
- Learners can review, retry, and reset.
- The final explanation is compared transparently; the platform does not claim to grade free text.
